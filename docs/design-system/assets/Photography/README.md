# Photography

Sportlab's own gym photos: a dark hall with black walls, warm OSB panels, overhead strip lights and real members and coaches. Use these instead of the dark placeholders.

| File | Format | What it shows | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `group-class-in-action.jpg` | Landscape 16:10 (2000×1250) | A group class mid-workout, shot low and wide: box jumps, air bike, kettlebell, dumbbells and jump rope under the arched roof. | Hero banners, blog overview hero, website headers, TV workout header. |
| `high-five-after-class.jpg` | Portrait (1990×2576) | Members high-fiving at the end of a class. | Community and "to keep going" moments, Instagram stories, cards. |
| `team-on-the-rowerg.jpg` | Landscape 3:2 (2576×1717) | Five people smiling around a RowErg and air bike, with the "ONE MORE" wall text and the gym clock behind them. | About us / team, group classes, feed posts. |
| `coaching-dumbbell-row.jpg` | Portrait (1990×2576) | A coach kneeling beside a member doing a dumbbell row, with a squat and the SPORTLAB banner in the background. | Personal training, instap / "to start", blog articles about technique. |

How to use them:

- **Text on a photo** is always `white`, on a `.sl-photo-overlay` gradient (transparent to `ink` at the bottom). Only the CTA pill is orange.
- **Crop for the format.** Keep faces and the action inside the safe area. Landscapes suit 16:9 or 16:10 heroes; portraits suit 9:16 stories and 4:5 posts.
- **Don't recolour** the photos, add filters or tint them orange. Their warm wood and dark walls already match the palette.
- **People:** these show real members and coaches. Use them for Sportlab's own channels only, and check consent before using them in paid ads.
